About us

Allan Gray Proprietary Limited is an authorised financial services provider with over five decades of investing experience.

Our investment approach

Our approach to investing is simple. We look closely at businesses. We buy shares we think are undervalued and sell them when we think they have reached their worth, regardless of popular opinion.

We pursue long-term returns without taking undue risks, and we invest with our clients' best interests at heart.

Our clients

All clients, from individuals to pension funds, get access to the same investment philosophy, process and ideas.

Privacy Policy

This page explains how Hairless Cats Home handles the personal information of people who visit this website or write to us about our kittens.

Information we receive

When you contact us, we receive the details you give us, such as your name, your email address and your questions about a kitten.

How we use it

We use this information only to answer your messages and to arrange adoption of a kitten with you. We do not use it for anything unrelated to that.

Your choices

If you would like us to correct or remove the details you have sent us, write to oursphynxhairlesskids@gmail.com and we will do so.

About Us

Who we are

Anmeshop is an online shop for babies and children. We offer clothing for boys and girls, baby accessories, baby toys, footwear, arts and crafts, novelties, bikes and tricycles, and prams and buggies.

What we sell

Our range is organised by category so that parents can find what they need quickly: Baby Boy, Baby Girl, Boy, Girl, Life Style, Discount, and Prams and Buggies. Many items are part of our clearance sale.

Our service

We offer free delivery on all clearance orders, and items can be returned within 30 days. Parents who shop with us can leave reviews, and we read every one of them to keep improving our range and our service.

Stay in touch

Subscribe to our newsletter at the bottom of any page to hear about special offers. If you have a question, visit our Contact Us page.
